设 $X$ 的分布列为
$$
\left( \begin{matrix}  - 2 &  - 1 & 0 & 1 & 2 \\  {0.15} & {0.2} & {0.2} & {0.2} & {0.25} \end{matrix}\right).
$$
试求以下随机变量的分布列
- $Y = {X}^{2}$, 
- $Z = {2X} - 1$, 
- $\Psi  = \left| X\right|  + 1$ 

## 解 
### $Y$
对于 $Y = {X}^{2}$, $Y$ 的取值为 4, 1 和 0, 而
$$
\begin{align}
P(Y = 4) &= P\left( \{ X = -2 \} \cup \{ X = 2 \} \right) \\
&= P(X = -2) + P(X = 2) \\
&= 0.15 + 0.25 = 0.4\text{.}
\end{align}
$$
$$
\begin{align}
P(Y = 1) &= P\left( \{ X = -1 \} \cup \{ X = 1 \} \right) \\
&= P(X = -1) + P(X = 1) \\
&= 0.2 + 0.2 = 0.4\text{.}
\end{align}
$$
$$
P\left( {Y = 0}\right)  = P\left( {X = 0}\right)  = {0.2}, 
$$

所以
$$
Y \sim  \left( \begin{matrix} 4 & 1 & 0 \\  {0.4} & {0.4} & {0.2} \end{matrix}\right).
$$
### $Z$
对于 $Z = {2X} - 1$, $Z$ 的取值为 $- 5, - 3, - 1, 1$ 和 3, 而
$$
\begin{align}
P(Z = -5) &= P(X = -2) = 0.15, \\
P(Z = -3) &= P(X = -1) = 0.2, \\
P(Z = -1) &= P(X = 0) = 0.2, \\
P(Z = 1)  &= P(X = 1) = 0.2, \\
P(Z = 3)  &= P(X = 2) = 0.25.
\end{align}
$$
所以
$$
Z \sim  \left( \begin{matrix}  - 5 &  - 3 &  - 1 & 1 & 3 \\  {0.15} & {0.2} & {0.2} & {0.2} & {0.25} \end{matrix}\right).
$$
### $\Psi$
对于 $\Psi  = \left| X\right|  + 1, \Psi$ 的取值为 3, 2, 和 1, 而
$$
\begin{align}
P(\Psi = 3) &= P\left( \{ X = -2 \} \cup \{ X = 2 \} \right) \\
&= P(X = -2) + P(X = 2) \\
&= 0.15 + 0.25 = 0.4\text{.}
\end{align}
$$
$$
\begin{align}
P(\Psi = 2) &= P\left( \{ X = -1 \} \cup \{ X = 1 \} \right) \\
&= P(X = -1) + P(X = 1) \\
&= 0.2 + 0.2 = 0.4\text{.}
\end{align}
$$
$$
P\left( {\Psi  = 1}\right)  = P\left( {X = 0}\right)  = {0.2}, 
$$
所以
$$
\Psi  \sim  \left( \begin{matrix} 3 & 2 & 1 \\  {0.4} & {0.4} & {0.2} \end{matrix}\right).
$$
