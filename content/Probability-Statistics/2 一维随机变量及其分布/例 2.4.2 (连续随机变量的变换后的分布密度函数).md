设 $X \sim  N\left( {0, 1}\right)$, 试求 $Y = {X}^{2}$ 的分布密度函数.

## 解 
我们先求 $Y = {X}^{2}$ 的分布函数 ${F}_{Y}$. 
显然, 对 $y < 0$ 有 $P\left( {{X}^{2} \leq  y}\right)  = 0$. 
对 $y \geq  0$, 有
$$
\begin{align}
F_Y(y) &= P\left( X^2 \leq y \right) \\
&= P\left( -\sqrt{y} \leq X \leq \sqrt{y} \right) \\
&= \int_{-\sqrt{y}}^{\sqrt{y}} \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} e^{-\frac{x^2}{2}} \, dx
\end{align}
$$

从而, 对 $y \geq  0$, 对 ${F}_{Y}\left( y\right)$ 关于 $y$ 求导, 得 $Y$ 的分布密度函数为
$$
\begin{align}
f_Y(y) &= \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} e^{-\frac{(\sqrt{y})^2}{2}} \times \left( \frac{1}{2\sqrt{y}} \right) \\
&\quad - \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} e^{-\frac{(-\sqrt{y})^2}{2}} \times \left( -\frac{1}{2\sqrt{y}} \right) \\
&= \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} \frac{1}{\sqrt{y}} e^{-\frac{y}{2}} \\
&= \frac{\left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^{\frac{1}{2}}}{\Gamma \left( \frac{1}{2} \right)} y^{-\frac{1}{2}} e^{-\frac{y}{2}}.
\end{align}
$$
其中用到 $\Gamma \left( \frac{1}{2}\right)  = \sqrt{\pi }$.

总之, 
$$
{f}_{Y}\left( y\right)  = \left\{  \begin{array}{ll} \frac{{\left( \frac{1}{2}\right) }^{\frac{1}{2}}}{\Gamma \left( \frac{1}{2}\right) }{y}^{-\frac{1}{2}}{\mathrm{e}}^{-\frac{y}{2}}, & y > 0, \\  0, & y \leq  0. \end{array}\right.
$$
