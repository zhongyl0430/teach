设 $X \sim  B\left( {{10}, {0.9}}\right)$, 试求 
- $P\left( {X = 8}\right)$, 
- $P\left( {X \leq  8}\right)$, 
- $P\left( {3 \leq  X \leq  9}\right)$.

## 解 
我们借助 $\mathrm{R}$ 软件来计算.
$$
\begin{align}
P\left( X = 8 \right) 
    &= \binom{10}{8} 0.9^8 \cdot 0.1^2 \\
    &= \operatorname{dbinom}(8, 10, 0.9) \\
    &= 0.1937102.
\end{align}
$$
$$
\begin{align}
P\left( X \leq 8 \right) 
    &= \sum_{k \leq 8} \binom{10}{k} 0.9^k \left( 1 - 0.9 \right)^{10 - k} \\
    &= \text{pbinom}(8, 10, 0.9) \\
    &= 0.2639011.
\end{align}
$$
$$
\begin{align}
P\left( 3 \leq X \leq 9 \right) 
    &= \sum_{3 \leq k \leq 9} \binom{10}{k} 0.9^k \left( 1 - 0.9 \right)^{10 - k} \\
    &= \operatorname{pbinom}(9, 10, 0.9) - \operatorname{pbinom}(2, 10, 0.9) \\
    &= 0.6513124.
\end{align}
$$
