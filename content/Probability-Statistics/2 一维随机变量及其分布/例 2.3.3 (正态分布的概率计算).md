设 $X \sim  N\left( {-1, 4}\right)$, 试求
- $P\left( {-5 \leq  X < 1}\right)$, 
- $P\left( {-2 \leq  X \leq  2}\right)$ 
- $P\left( {\left| X\right|  < 1}\right)$, 
- $P\left( {\left| X\right|  \geq  \frac{3}{2}}\right).$

## 解 
由题设, $\mu  =  - 1, \sigma  = 2$, 由 (2.3.12), (2.3.13) 和 $\mathrm{R}$ 软件中的函数 pnorm, 
$$
\begin{align}
P\left( -5 \leq X < 1 \right) &= \Phi \left( \frac{1 - (-1)}{2} \right) - \Phi \left( \frac{-5 - (-1)}{2} \right) \\
&= \Phi(1) - \Phi(-2) \\
&= \Phi(1) + \Phi(2) - 1 \\
&= \operatorname{pnorm}(1) + \operatorname{pnorm}(2) - 1 \\
&= 0.8185946
\end{align}
$$
$$
\begin{align}
P\left( -2 \leq X \leq 2 \right) &= \Phi \left( \frac{2 - (-1)}{2} \right) - \Phi \left( \frac{-2 - (-1)}{2} \right) \\
&= \Phi \left( \frac{3}{2} \right) - \Phi \left( -\frac{1}{2} \right) \\
&= \operatorname{pnorm}\left( \frac{3}{2} \right) - \operatorname{pnorm}\left( -\frac{1}{2} \right) \\
&= 0.6246553
\end{align}
$$
$$
\begin{align}
P(|X| < 1) 
&= \Phi \left( \frac{1 - (-1)}{2} \right) - \Phi \left( \frac{-1 - (-1)}{2} \right) \\
&= \Phi(1) - \Phi(0) \\
&= \operatorname{pnorm}(1) - \operatorname{pnorm}(0) \\
&= 0.3413447.
\end{align}
$$
$$
\begin{align}
P\left( |X| \geq \frac{3}{2} \right) 
&= P\left( X \geq \frac{3}{2} \right) + P\left( X \leq -\frac{3}{2} \right) \\
&= 1 - \Phi \left( \frac{\frac{3}{2} - (-1)}{2} \right) + \Phi \left( \frac{-\frac{3}{2} - (-1)}{2} \right) \\
&= 1 - \Phi \left( \frac{5}{4} \right) + \Phi \left( -\frac{1}{4} \right) \\
&= 1 - \operatorname{pnorm} \left( \frac{5}{4} \right) + \operatorname{pnorm} \left( -\frac{1}{4} \right) \\
&= 0.5069434.
\end{align}
$$
