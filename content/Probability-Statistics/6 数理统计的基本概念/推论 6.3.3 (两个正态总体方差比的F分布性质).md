- 设以下两个独立的随机变量 ^list
	- 总体 $X \sim  N\left( {{\mu }_{1},{\sigma }_{1}^{2}}\right)$, $\left( {{X}_{1},{X}_{2},\cdots,{X}_{m}}\right)$ 为其样本. 
		- 样本均值为 $X$, 样本方差为 ${S}_{1m}^{2}$; 
	- 总体 $Y \sim  N\left( {{\mu }_{2},{\sigma }_{2}^{2}}\right)$, $\left( {{Y}_{1},{Y}_{2},\cdots,{Y}_{m}}\right)$ 为其样本, 
		- 样本均值为 $\bar{Y}$, 样本方差为 ${S}_{2m}^{2}$. 

则
$$
\frac{m{S}_{1m}^{2}}{n{S}_{2n}^{2}} \cdot  \frac{{\sigma }_{2}^{2}}{{\sigma }_{1}^{2}} \cdot  \frac{n - 1}{m - 1} = \frac{{S}_{1m}^{*2}}{{S}_{2n}^{*2}} \cdot  \frac{{\sigma }_{2}^{2}}{{\sigma }_{1}^{2}} \sim  F\left( {m - 1, n - 1}\right). \tag{6.3.15}
$$
