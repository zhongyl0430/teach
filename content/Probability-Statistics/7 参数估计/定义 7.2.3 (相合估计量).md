设总体 $X \sim  {F}_{X}\left( {\cdot,\theta }\right),\theta  \in  \Theta$. 并设 $T\left( {{X}_{1},{X}_{2},\cdots,{X}_{n}}\right)$ 为 $g\left( \theta \right)$ 的估计量,如果对任意 $\varepsilon  > 0$ 有
$$
\mathop{\lim }\limits_{{n \rightarrow  \infty }}P\left( {\left| {T\left( {{X}_{1},{X}_{2},\cdots,{X}_{n}}\right)  - g\left( \theta \right) }\right|  \geq  \varepsilon }\right)  = 0,\;\forall \theta  \in  \Theta. \tag{7.2.3}
$$
则称 $T\left( {{X}_{1},{X}_{2},\cdots,{X}_{n}}\right)$ 为 $g\left( \theta \right)$ 的**相合估计量**.
