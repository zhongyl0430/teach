基于![[定理 6.3.1 (抽样分布基本定理)#^statement]]
我们还可以得到正态总体 $N\left( {\mu,{\sigma }^{2}}\right)$ 中参数 $\mu$ 和 ${\sigma }^{2}$ 的联合区间估计.

事实上, 若
$$
P\left( {\left| \frac{\bar{X} - \mu }{\sqrt{\frac{{\sigma }^{2}}{n}}}\right|  \leq  l,{k}_{1} \leq  \frac{n{S}_{n}^{2}}{{\sigma }^{2}} \leq  {k}_{2}}\right)  = 1 - \alpha,
$$

由于 $\bar{X}$ 与 ${S}_{n}^{2}$ 独立,则有
$$
P\left( {\left| \frac{\bar{X} - \mu }{\sqrt{\frac{{\sigma }^{2}}{n}}}\right|  \leq  l}\right)  \cdot  P\left( {{k}_{1} \leq  \frac{n{S}_{n}^{2}}{{\sigma }^{2}} \leq  {k}_{2}}\right)  = 1 - \alpha.
$$

若令
 - $P\left( {\left| \frac{\bar{X} - \mu }{\sqrt{\frac{{\sigma }^{2}}{n}}}\right|  \leq  l}\right)  = \sqrt{1 - \alpha }$
 - $P\left( {{k}_{1} \leq  \frac{n{S}_{n}^{2}}{{\sigma }^{2}} \leq  {k}_{2}}\right)  = \sqrt{1 - \alpha }$

则有
- $P\left( {\frac{n{\left( \bar{X} - \mu \right) }^{2}}{{l}^{2}} \leq  {\sigma }^{2}}\right)  = \sqrt{1 - \alpha }$
- $P\left( {\frac{n{S}_{n}^{2}}{{k}_{2}} \leq  {\sigma }^{2} \leq  \frac{n{S}_{n}^{2}}{{k}_{1}}}\right)  = \sqrt{1 - \alpha }$

由于
- $\frac{\bar{X} - \mu }{\sqrt{\frac{{\sigma }^{2}}{n}}} \sim  N\left( {0,1}\right)$
- $\frac{n{S}_{n}^{2}}{{\sigma }^{2}} \sim  {\chi }^{2}\left( {n - 1}\right)$

可查表得
- $l = {u}_{\frac{1}{2}\left( {1 + \sqrt{1 - \alpha }}\right) }$,
- ${k}_{1} = {\chi }_{\frac{1}{2}\left( {1 - \sqrt{1 - \alpha }}\right) }^{2}\left( {n - 1}\right)$,
- ${k}_{2} = {\chi }_{\frac{1}{2}\left( {1 + \sqrt{1 - \alpha }}\right) }^{2}\left( {n - 1}\right)$. 

最终我们得到 $\left( {\mu,{\sigma }^{2}}\right)$ 的置信度为 $1 - \alpha$ 联合区间估计,
$$
\left\{ \left( {\mu,{\sigma}^{2}} \right)  : 
\begin{align*}
\frac{n{\left( \bar{X} - \mu \right) }^{2}}{{u}_{\frac{1}{2}\left( {1 + \sqrt{1 - \alpha }}\right) }^{2}} &\leq  {\sigma }^{2}, \\
\frac{n{S}_{n}^{2}}{{\chi }_{\frac{1}{2}\left( {1 + \sqrt{1 - \alpha }}\right) }^{2}\left( {n - 1}\right) } 
&\leq  {\sigma }^{2} 
\leq  \frac{n{S}_{n}^{2}}{{\chi }_{\frac{1}{2}\left( {1 - \sqrt{1 - \alpha }}\right) }^{2}\left( {n - 1}\right) }
\end{align*}
\right\}.
$$
