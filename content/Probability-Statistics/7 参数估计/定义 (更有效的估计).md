如果 ${T}_{1}\left( {{X}_{1},{X}_{2},\cdots,{X}_{n}}\right)$ 和 ${T}_{2}\left( {{X}_{1},{X}_{2},\cdots,{X}_{n}}\right)$ 都是待估计参数 $g\left( \theta \right)$ 的无偏估计, 但
$${\operatorname{Var}}_{\theta }\left\lbrack  {T}_{1}\right\rbrack   \leq  {\operatorname{Var}}_{\theta }\left\lbrack  {T}_{2}\right\rbrack ,$$
我们称 ${T}_{1}$ 比 ${T}_{2}$ **有效**.
