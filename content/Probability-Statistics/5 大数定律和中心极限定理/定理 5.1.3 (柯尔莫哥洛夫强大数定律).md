设 ${X}_{1},{X}_{2},\cdots$ 为独立随机变量序列, 具有有限的数学期望, 且 $\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{n = 1}}^{\infty }\frac{\operatorname{Var}\left\lbrack  {X}_{n}\right\rbrack  }{{n}^{2}} < \infty$,则
$$
P\left( {\mathop{\lim }\limits_{{n \rightarrow  \infty }}{n}^{-1}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{k = 1}}^{n}\left( {{X}_{k} - E\left\lbrack  {X}_{k}\right\rbrack  }\right)  = 0}\right)  = 1.
$$
