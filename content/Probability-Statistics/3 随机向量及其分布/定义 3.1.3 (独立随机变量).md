设 $\left( {\Omega,\mathcal{F}, P}\right)$ 为概率空间, ${X}_{1},{X}_{2},\cdots,{X}_{n}$ 为其上的随机变量, 如果
$$
\begin{align}
F_{X_1, X_2, \cdots, X_n}(x_1, x_2, \cdots, x_n) 
    &= F_{X_1}(x_1) F_{X_2}(x_2) \cdots F_{X_n}(x_n), \quad \forall (x_1, x_2, \cdots, x_n) \in \mathbb{R}^n, \tag{3.1.6}
\end{align}
$$
则称 ${X}_{1},{X}_{2},\cdots,{X}_{n}$ **相互独立**.
