## 3.1.

## 3.2. 
设(X, Y)的分布密度函数为
$$
f\left( {x, y}\right)  = \left\{  \begin{array}{ll} {Axy}, & 0 < x < 1,0 < y < 1, \\  0, & \text{ 其他. } \end{array}\right.
$$

求 (1) 常数 $A$. (2) $P\left( {X < {0.4}, Y < {1.3}}\right)$.
