设 $\left( {\Omega,\mathcal{F}, P}\right)$ 为概率空间, $\left( {{X}_{1},{X}_{2},\cdots,{X}_{n}}\right)$ 为其上的随机向量, 它的联合分布函数定义为
$$
\begin{align}
&F_{X_1, X_2, \cdots, X_n}\left( x_1, x_2, \cdots, x_n \right) \\
    &= P\left( \omega \in \Omega : X_1(\omega) \leq x_1, X_2(\omega) \leq x_2, \cdots, X_n(\omega) \leq x_n \right) \\
    &= P\left( \omega \in \Omega : \bigcap_{i = 1}^{n} \left\{ X_i(\omega) \leq x_i \right\} \right), \quad \forall (x_1, x_2, \cdots, x_n) \in \mathbb{R}^n. \tag{3.1.1}
\end{align}
$$
