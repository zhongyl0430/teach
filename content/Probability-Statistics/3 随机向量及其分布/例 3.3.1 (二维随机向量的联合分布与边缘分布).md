已知二维随机向量 $(X, Y)$ 的联合分布密度函数为
$$
{f}_{X, Y}\left( {x, y}\right)  = \left\{  \begin{array}{ll} {kxy}, & {x}^{2} \leq  y \leq  1,0 \leq  x \leq  1, \\  0, & \text{ 其他. } \end{array}\right.
$$
1. 试确定 $k$ 的值.
2. 试求 $(X, Y)$ 落在区域 $D = \left\{  {\left( {x, y}\right)  \mid  {x}^{2} \leq  y \leq  x,0 \leq  x \leq  1}\right\}$ 的概率.
3. 试求 $X$ 和 $Y$ 的边缘分布密度函数.
4. 试问 $X$ 与 $Y$ 是否相互独立?

## 解 
### 1
由于
$$
\begin{align}
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} f_{X,Y}(x, y) \,dx\,dy 
&= \int_{0}^{1} dx \int_{x^2}^{1} kxy \,dy \\
&= k \int_{0}^{1} x \left( \frac{1}{2} - \frac{x^4}{2} \right) dx \\
&= \frac{k}{6}
\end{align}
$$

由 (3.3.1) 知 $k = 6$.
代入得
$$
{f}_{X, Y}\left( {x, y}\right)  = \left\{  
\begin{array}{ll} {6xy}, & {x}^{2} \leq  y \leq  1,0 \leq  x \leq  1, \\  0, & \text{ 其他. } \end{array}
\right.
$$

^eq-def

### 2
由 (3.3.2) 有$$
\begin{align}
P\left( (X, Y) \in D \right) 
&= \iint_{D} f_{X, Y}(x, y) \,dx\,dy \\
&= \iint_{D} 6xy \,dx\,dy \\
&= \int_{0}^{1} dx \int_{x^2}^{x} 6xy \,dy \\
&= \int_{0}^{1} 3x \left(x^2 - x^4 \right) dx \\
&= \frac{1}{4}.
\end{align}
$$
### 3
$$
\begin{align}
f_X(x) 
&= \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} f_{X, Y}(x, y) \,dy \\
&= 6x \int_{x^2}^{1} y \,dy \\
&= 6x \left(\frac{1}{2} - \frac{x^4}{2}\right) \\
&= 3x (1 - x^4), \quad 0 \leq x \leq 1.
\end{align}
$$

$$
\begin{align}
f_Y(y) 
&= \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} f_{X, Y}(x, y) \,dx \\
&= 6y \int_{0}^{\sqrt{y}} x \,dx \\
&= 3y^2, \quad 0 \leq y \leq 1.
\end{align}
$$

### 4
由 (3) 的结果知, ${f}_{X, Y}\left( {x, y}\right)  \neq  {f}_{X}\left( x\right) {f}_{Y}\left( y\right)$, 所以 $X$ 与 $Y$ 不相互独立.
