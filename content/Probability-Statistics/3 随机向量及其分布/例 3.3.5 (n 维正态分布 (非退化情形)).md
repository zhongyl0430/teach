设 $\mathbf{\mu } = {\left( {\mu }_{1},{\mu }_{2},\cdots,{\mu }_{n}\right) }^{\prime }$, $\Sigma$ 为 $n$ 阶正定矩阵,记 $\mathbf{X} = {\left( {x}_{1},{x}_{2},\cdots,{x}_{n}\right) }^{\prime }$ 若
$$
{f}_{{X}_{1},{X}_{2},\cdots,{X}_{n}}\left( {{x}_{1},{x}_{2}\cdots,{x}_{n}}\right)  = \frac{1}{{\left( 2\pi \right) }^{\frac{n}{2}}{\left| \Sigma \right| }^{\frac{n}{2}}}\exp \left\{  {-\frac{1}{2}{\left( \mathbf{X} - \mathbf{\mu }\right) }^{\prime } \Sigma^{-1}\left( {\mathbf{X} - \mathbf{\mu }}\right) }\right\} .
$$
则称 ${\left( {X}_{1},{X}_{2},\cdots,{X}_{n}\right) }^{\prime }$ 服从 $n$ 维正态分布, 记作 $\mathbf{X} \sim  N\left( {\mathbf{\mu },\Sigma}\right)$.

不难看出, 在例 3.3.5 的二维正态分布中,
$$
\mathbf{\mu } = \left( \begin{array}{l} {\mu }_{1} \\  {\mu }_{2} \end{array}\right),\; \Sigma = \left( \begin{array}{ll} {\sigma }_{1}^{2} & \rho {\sigma }_{1}{\sigma }_{2} \\  \rho {\sigma }_{1}{\sigma }_{2} & {\sigma }_{2}^{2} \end{array}\right).
$$
