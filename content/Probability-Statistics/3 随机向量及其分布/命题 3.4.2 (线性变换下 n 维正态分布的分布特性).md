设随机向量 $\mathbf{X} = {\left( {X}_{1},{X}_{2},\cdots,{X}_{n}\right) }^{\prime }$ 服从 $n$ 维正态分布, 
- 期望向量为 $\mathbf{\mu } = {\left( {\mu }_{1},{\mu }_{2},\cdots,{\mu }_{n}\right) }^{\prime }$, 
- 协方差矩阵为 $\Sigma$, 

则对任意实数矩阵 ${\mathbf{A}}_{m \times  n}$, 有 $$\mathbf{{AX}} \sim  N\left( {\mathbf{{A\mu }},\mathbf{{A}} \Sigma {\mathbf{A}}^{\prime }}\right).$$
