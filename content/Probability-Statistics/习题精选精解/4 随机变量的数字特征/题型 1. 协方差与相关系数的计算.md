# 3.1
设随机变量 $X$ 和 $Y$ 的联合概率分布为

| Y \ X | -1   | 0    | 1    |
|-------|------|------|------|
| 0     | 0.07 | 0.18 | 0.15 |
| 1     | 0.08 | 0.32 | 0.20 |
则 $X$ 和 $Y$ 的相关系数 $\rho  =$ . 
${X}^{2}$ 和 ${Y}^{2}$ 的协方差 $\operatorname{Cov}\left( {{X}^{2},{Y}^{2}}\right)  =$ .

## 解
### 1
$X$ 的分布律

| X   | 0   | 1   |
|-----|-----|-----|
| P   | 0.4 | 0.6 |

$Y$ 的分布律

| Y   | -1   | 0    | 1    |
|-----|------|------|------|
| P   | 0.15 | 0.5  | 0.35 |

$$
E\left( X\right)  = {0.6},
$$
$$
E\left( Y\right)  = {0.35} - {0.15} = {0.2},
$$
$$
E\left( {XY}\right)  = \mathop{\sum }\limits_{i}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{j}{x}_{i}{y}_{j}{p}_{ij} =  - {0.08} + {0.20} = {0.12},
$$
所以
$$
\operatorname{Cov}\left( {X, Y}\right)  = E\left( {XY}\right)  - E\left( X\right) E\left( Y\right)  = 0.
$$
### 2
$$
E\left( {Y}^{2}\right)  = {0.5},
$$
$$
E\left( {{X}^{2}{Y}^{2}}\right)  = \mathop{\sum }\limits_{i}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{j}{x}_{i}^{2}{y}_{j}^{2}{p}_{ij} = {0.28}.
$$
所以
$$\operatorname{Cov}\left( {{X}^{2},{Y}^{2}}\right)  = E\left( {{X}^{2}{Y}^{2}}\right)  - E\left( {X}^{2}\right) E\left( {Y}^{2}\right)  =  - {0.02}.$$

# 3.2
已知 $X \sim  \left\lbrack  \begin{matrix}  - 1 & 1 \\  \frac{1}{2} & \frac{1}{2} \end{matrix}\right\rbrack$, $Y \sim  \left\lbrack  \begin{matrix} 0 & 1 \\  \frac{1}{4} & \frac{3}{4} \end{matrix}\right\rbrack$, $P\{ X = Y\}  = \frac{1}{4}$, 则 $r (X, Y) =$ ___ .

## 解
由 $P\{ X = Y\}  = P\{ X = 1, Y = 1\}  = \frac{1}{4}$,可求得 $\left( {X, Y}\right)$ 联合分布律

| Y   | 0         | 1         |
|-----|-----------|-----------|
| -1  | 0         | $\frac{1}{2}$ |
| 1   | $\frac{1}{4}$ | $\frac{1}{4}$ |

故 $E\left( {XY}\right)  =  - \frac{1}{4}$, 又
$$
{EX} = 0,\;{DX} = 1,\;{EY} = \frac{3}{4},\;{DY} = \frac{3}{16},
$$
则 
$$
\begin{aligned}
{\rho }_{XY} &= \frac{\operatorname{Cov}\left( {X, Y}\right) }{\sqrt{DX} \cdot  \sqrt{DY}} \\
&= \frac{E\left( {XY}\right)  - {EX} \cdot  {EY}}{\sqrt{DX} \cdot  \sqrt{DY}} \\
&=  - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}
\end{aligned}
$$

# 3.3
设随机变量 $\left( {X, Y}\right)$ 具有概率密度函数
$$
f\left( {x, y}\right)  = \left\{  \begin{array}{ll} \frac{1}{8}\left( {x + y}\right), & 0 \leq  x \leq  2,0 \leq  y \leq  2 \\  0, & \text{ 其他 } \end{array}\right.
$$

求 $E\left( X\right), E\left( Y\right),\operatorname{Cov}\left( {X, Y}\right),{\rho }_{XY}, D\left( {X + Y}\right)$.

## 解
由于
$$
\begin{aligned}
E\left( X\right)  &= {\int }_{-\infty }^{+\infty }{\int }_{-\infty }^{+\infty }{xf}\left( {x, y}\right) \mathrm{d}x\mathrm{\;d}y \\
&= {\int }_{0}^{2}\mathrm{\;d}x{\int }_{0}^{2}\frac{1}{8}x\left( {x + y}\right) \mathrm{d}y \\
&= \frac{7}{6}
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
E\left( Y\right)  &= {\int }_{-\infty }^{+\infty }{\int }_{-\infty }^{+\infty }{yf}\left( {x, y}\right) \mathrm{d}x\mathrm{\;d}y \\
&= {\int }_{0}^{2}\mathrm{\;d}x{\int }_{0}^{2}\frac{1}{8}y\left( {x + y}\right) \mathrm{d}y \\
&= \frac{7}{6}
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
E\left( {X}^{2}\right)  &= {\int }_{-\infty }^{+\infty }{\int }_{-\infty }^{+\infty }{x}^{2}f\left( {x, y}\right) \mathrm{d}x\mathrm{\;d}y \\
&= {\int }_{0}^{2}\mathrm{\;d}x{\int }_{0}^{2}\frac{1}{8}{x}^{2}\left( {x + y}\right) \mathrm{d}y \\
&= \frac{10}{6}
\end{aligned}
$$
同理 $E\left( {Y}^{2}\right)  = \frac{10}{6}$ 故
$$
\begin{aligned}
D\left( X\right)  &= E\left( {X}^{2}\right)  - {\left( EX\right) }^{2} \\
&= \frac{10}{6} - \frac{49}{36} \\
&= \frac{11}{36}.
\end{aligned}
$$
同理 ${DY} = \frac{11}{36}$.
$$
\begin{aligned}
E\left( {XY}\right)  &= {\int }_{-\infty }^{+\infty }{\int }_{-\infty }^{+\infty }{xyf}\left( {x, y}\right) \mathrm{d}x\mathrm{\;d}y \\
&= {\int }_{0}^{2}\mathrm{\;d}x{\int }_{0}^{2}\frac{1}{8}{xy}\left( {x + y}\right) \mathrm{d}y \\
&= \frac{8}{6}
\end{aligned}
$$
故
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Cov}\left( {X, Y}\right)  &= E\left( {XY}\right)  - {EX} \cdot  {EY} \\
&= \frac{8}{6} - \frac{49}{36} \\
&=  - \frac{1}{36}
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
{\rho }_{XY} &= \frac{\operatorname{Cov}\left( {X, Y}\right) }{\sqrt{DX} \cdot  \sqrt{DY}} \\
&=  - \frac{\frac{1}{36}}{\sqrt{\frac{11}{36} \cdot  \frac{11}{36}}} \\
&=  - \frac{1}{11}
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
D\left( {X + Y}\right)  &= {DX} + {DY} + 2\operatorname{Cov}\left( {X, Y}\right)  \\
&= \frac{11}{36} + \frac{11}{36} - \frac{2}{36} \\
&= \frac{5}{9}.
\end{aligned}
$$
# 3.4
某箱装有 100 件产品,其中一、二和三等品分别为 80、10 和 10 件,现在从中随机抽取一件, 记

$$
{X}_{i} = \left\{  {\begin{array}{ll} 1, & \text{ 若抽到 }i\text{ 等品 } \\  0, & \text{ 其他 } \end{array}\;\left( {i = 1,2,3}\right) }\right.
$$

试求 (1) 随机变量 ${X}_{1}$ 和 ${X}_{2}$ 的联合分布;

(2)随机变量 ${X}_{1}$ 与 ${X}_{2}$ 的相关系数 $\rho$.

## 解
(1) 设事件 ${A}_{i} =$ “抽到 $i$ 等品” $\left( {i = 1,2,3}\right)$. 由题意知 ${A}_{1},{A}_{2},{A}_{3}$ 两两互不相容.

$P\left( {A}_{1}\right)  = {0.8},\;P\left( {A}_{2}\right)  = P\left( {A}_{3}\right)  = {0.1}.$

易见,

$P\left\{  {{X}_{1} = 0,{X}_{2} = 0}\right\}   = P\left( {A}_{3}\right)  = {0.1},$

$P\left\{  {{X}_{1} = 0,{X}_{2} = 1}\right\}   = P\left( {A}_{2}\right)  = {0.1};$

$P\left\{  {{X}_{1} = 1,{X}_{2} = 0}\right\}   = P\left( {A}_{1}\right)  = {0.8},$

$P\left\{  {{X}_{1} = 1,{X}_{2} = 1}\right\}   = P\left( \varnothing \right)  = 0.$

故 ${X}_{1}$ 和 ${X}_{2}$ 的联合分布为

| ${X}_{1}$ ${X}_{2}$ | 0   | 1   |
|-----------------------|-----|-----|
| 0                     | 0.1 | 0.8 |
| 1                     | 0.1 | 0   |
(2) $E{X}_{1} = {0.8},\;E{X}_{2} = {0.1}$,

$D{X}_{1} = {0.8} \times  {0.2} = {0.16},\;D{X}_{2} = {0.1} \times  {0.9} = {0.09},$

$E\left( {{X}_{1}{X}_{2}}\right)  = 0 \times  0 \times  {0.1} + 0 \times  1 \times  {0.1} + 1 \times  0 \times  {0.8} + 1 \times  1 \times  0 = 0,$

$\operatorname{Cov}\left( {{X}_{1},{X}_{2}}\right)  = E\left( {{X}_{1}{X}_{2}}\right)  - E{X}_{1} \cdot  E{X}_{2} = 0 - {0.8} \times  {0.1} =  - {0.08},$

$\rho  = \frac{\operatorname{Cov}\left( {{X}_{1},{X}_{2}}\right) }{\sqrt{D{X}_{1} \cdot  D{X}_{2}}} = \frac{-{0.08}}{\sqrt{{0.16} \times  {0.09}}} =  - \frac{2}{3}.$
