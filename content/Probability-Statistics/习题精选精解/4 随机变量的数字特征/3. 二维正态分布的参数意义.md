### 3. 二维正态分布的参数意义

当 $\left( {X, Y}\right)  \sim  N\left( {{\mu }_{1},{\sigma }_{1}^{2};{\mu }_{2},{\sigma }_{2}^{2};\rho }\right)$ 时,
$$
{EX} = {\mu }_{1},\;{EY} = {\mu }_{2},\;{DX} = {\sigma }_{1}^{2},\;{DY} = {\sigma }_{2}^{2},\;{\rho }_{XY} = \rho.
$$

且 $X, Y$ 相互独立 $\Leftrightarrow  X\text{、}Y$ 不相关.
