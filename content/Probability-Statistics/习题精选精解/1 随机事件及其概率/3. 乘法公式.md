设 $A, B$ 是任意两个随机事件, $P\left( A\right)  > 0, P\left( B\right)  > 0$,则
$$
P\left( {AB}\right)  = P\left( {A \mid  B}\right) P\left( B\right)  = P\left( {B \mid  A}\right) P\left( A\right).
$$

一般地,设 ${A}_{1},\cdots,{A}_{n}$ 是 $n$ 个随机事件,且 $P\left( {{A}_{1}\cdots {A}_{n - 1}}\right)  > 0$,则
$$
P\left( {{A}_{1}\cdots {A}_{n}}\right)  = P\left( {{A}_{n} \mid  {A}_{1}\cdots {A}_{n - 1}}\right) \cdots P\left( {{A}_{3} \mid  {A}_{1}{A}_{2}}\right) P\left( {{A}_{2} \mid  {A}_{1}}\right) P\left( {A}_{1}\right).
$$
