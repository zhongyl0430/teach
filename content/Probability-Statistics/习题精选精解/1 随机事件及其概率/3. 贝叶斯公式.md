设事件 ${B}_{1},{B}_{2},\cdots,{B}_{n}$ 是样本空间 $\Omega$ 的一个分划, $P\left( {B}_{i}\right)  > 0\left( {i = 1,2,\cdots, n}\right), A$ 为试验的任一事件. 且 $P\left( A\right)  > 0$,则有
$$
P\left( {{B}_{i} \mid  A}\right)  = \frac{P\left( {B}_{i}\right) P\left( {A \mid  {B}_{i}}\right) }{\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{j = 1}}^{n}P\left( {B}_{j}\right) P\left( {A \mid  {B}_{j}}\right) }\;\left( {i = 1,2,\cdots, n}\right)
$$
