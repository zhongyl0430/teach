设 $X$ 的概率分布为
$$
X \sim  \left( \begin{matrix}  - 1 & 0 & 1 & 3 \\  \frac{1}{8} & \frac{1}{4} & \frac{3}{8} & \frac{1}{4} \end{matrix}\right).
$$

试求 $E\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack， E\left\lbrack  {-X + 2}\right\rbrack$ 和 $E\left\lbrack  {X}^{2}\right\rbrack$.

## 解 
$$
\begin{align}
E[X] &= (-1) \times \frac{1}{8} + 0 \times \frac{1}{4} + 1 \times \frac{3}{8} + 3 \times \frac{1}{4} = 1, \\
E[-X + 2] &= (1 + 2) \times \frac{1}{8} + (0 + 2) \times \frac{1}{4} + (-1 + 2) \times \frac{3}{8} + (-3 + 2) \times \frac{1}{4} = 1, \\
E[X^2] &= (-1)^2 \times \frac{1}{8} + 0^2 \times \frac{1}{4} + 1^2 \times \frac{3}{8} + 3^2 \times \frac{1}{4} = \frac{11}{4}.
\end{align}
$$
