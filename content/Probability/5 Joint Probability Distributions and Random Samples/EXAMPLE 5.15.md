The joint and marginal pmf’s for 
- $X$ = automobile policy deductible amount
- $Y$ = homeowner policy deductible amount in [[EXAMPLE 5.1]]

were

| $p(x, y)$ | 500 | 1000 | 5000 | 
| --------- | --- | ---- | ---- | 
| 100       | .30 | .05  | 0    | 
| 500       | .15 | .20  | .05  | 
| 1000      | .10 | .10  | .05  | 

|$x$          |100 | 500 | 1000 | 
|------------ | --- | --- | ---- | 
|${p}_{X}(x)$ |  .35 | .40 | .25  |

| $y$          |500 | 1000 | 5000 |
|------------ | --- | ---- | ---- |
| ${p}_{Y}(y)$ | .55 | .35  | .10  |

from which ${\mu }_{X} = {\sum x}{p}_{X}\left( x\right) = {485}$ and ${\mu }_{Y} = {1125}$. 
Therefore,
$$
\begin{align}
\operatorname{Cov}\left( X, Y \right) 
&= \sum_{(x, y)} \left( x - 485 \right) \left( y - 1125 \right) p\left( x, y \right) \\
&= \left( 100 - 485 \right) \left( 500 - 1125 \right) \left( 0.30 \right) + \ldots \\
&\quad + \left( 1000 - 485 \right) \left( 5000 - 1125 \right) \left( 0.05 \right) \\
&= 136,875.
\end{align}
$$
