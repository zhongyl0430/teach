Let $X$ and $Y$ be jointly distributed rv’s with $\operatorname{pmf}p\left( {x, y}\right)$ or $\operatorname{pdf}f\left( {x, y}\right)$ according to whether the variables are discrete or continuous. 
Then the expected value of a function $h\left( {X, Y}\right)$, denoted by $E\left\lbrack {h\left( {X, Y}\right) }\right\rbrack$ or ${\mu }_{h\left( {X, Y}\right) }$, is given by
$$
\left\{ \begin{array}{ll} \mathop{\sum }\limits_{x}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{y}h\left( {x, y}\right) \cdot p\left( {x, y}\right) & \text{ if }X\text{ and }Y\text{ are discrete } \\ {\int }_{-\infty }^{\infty }{\int }_{-\infty }^{\infty }h\left( {x, y}\right) \cdot f\left( {x, y}\right) {dxdy} & \text{ if }X\text{ and }Y\text{ are continuous } \end{array}\right.
$$
