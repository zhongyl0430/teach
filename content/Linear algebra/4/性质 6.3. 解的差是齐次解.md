> [!proposition] 性质 6.3. 解的差是齐次解
> 设 ${\gamma }_{1},{\gamma }_{2}$ 是 $\mathbf{A}\mathbf{X} = \mathbf{b}$ 的解,则 ${\gamma }_{1} - {\gamma }_{2}$ 是 $\mathbf{A}\mathbf{X} = \mathbf{0}$ 的解.

`\begin{proof}`
由 $\mathbf{A}{\gamma }_{1} = \mathbf{b}$, $\mathbf{A}{\gamma }_{2} = \mathbf{b}$, 可得 $\mathbf{A}\left( {{\gamma }_{1} - {\gamma }_{2}}\right) = \mathbf{A}{\gamma }_{1} - \mathbf{A}{\gamma }_{2} = \mathbf{b} - \mathbf{b} = \mathbf{0}$ .
`\end{proof}`
