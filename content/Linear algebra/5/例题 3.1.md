> [!question] 
> 设 ${\alpha }_{1},{\alpha }_{2},\cdots,{\alpha }_{n}$ 为向量空间 ${\mathbb{R}}^{n}$ 的任意一组标准正交基, $\alpha \in {\mathbb{R}}^{n},\mathbf{\alpha } = {k}_{1}{\mathbf{\alpha }}_{1} + {k}_{2}{\mathbf{\alpha }}_{2} + \cdots + {k}_{n}{\mathbf{\alpha }}_{n}$,
> 证明: ${k}_{i} = \left( {{\mathbf{\alpha }}_{i},\mathbf{\alpha }}\right), i = 1,2,\cdots, n$.

# 证明 
用 ${\mathbf{\alpha }}_{i}\left( {i = 1,2,\cdots, n}\right)$ 与 $\mathbf{\alpha }$ 作内积,由于 $\left( {{\mathbf{\alpha }}_{i},{\mathbf{\alpha }}_{j}}\right) = 0\left( {i \neq j}\right),\left( {{\mathbf{\alpha }}_{i},{\mathbf{\alpha }}_{i}}\right) =$ 1, 得到
$$
\begin{align}
\left( {{\mathbf{\alpha }}_{i},\mathbf{\alpha }}\right) &= \left( {{\mathbf{\alpha }}_{i},{k}_{1}{\mathbf{\alpha }}_{1} + {k}_{2}{\mathbf{\alpha }}_{2} + \cdots + {k}_{n}{\mathbf{\alpha }}_{n}}\right) \\
&= \left( {{\mathbf{\alpha }}_{i},{k}_{i}{\mathbf{\alpha }}_{i}}\right) = {k}_{i}\left( {{\mathbf{\alpha }}_{i},{\mathbf{\alpha }}_{i}}\right) = {k}_{i}.
\end{align}
$$
