设 $\mathbf{\alpha } = \left( {{x}_{1},{x}_{2},\cdots,{x}_{n}}\right)$, $\mathbf{\beta } = \left( {{y}_{1},{y}_{2},\cdots,{y}_{n}}\right) \in {\mathbb{R}}^{n}$, 定义
$$
\left( {\mathbf{\alpha },\mathbf{\beta }}\right) = \mathbf{\alpha }{\mathbf{\beta }}^{\mathrm{T}} = {x}_{1}{y}_{1} + {x}_{2}{y}_{2} + \cdots + {x}_{n}{y}_{n} = \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 1}}^{n}{x}_{i}{y}_{i}
$$
称 $\left( {\mathbf{\alpha },\mathbf{\beta }}\right)$ 为 $\mathbf{\alpha },\mathbf{\beta }$ 的**内积**.
