若 $\mathbf{\alpha } = \left( {{a}_{1},{a}_{2},\cdots,{a}_{n}}\right) \in {\mathbb{R}}^{n}$, 实数 $\left| \mathbf{\alpha }\right| = \sqrt{\left( \mathbf{\alpha },\mathbf{\alpha }\right) }$ 称为 $\mathbf{\alpha }$ 的**长度**, 即
$$
\left| \mathbf{\alpha }\right| = \sqrt{\left( \mathbf{\alpha },\mathbf{\alpha }\right) } = \sqrt{{a}_{1}^{2} + {a}_{2}^{2} + \cdots + {a}_{n}^{2}}.
$$
