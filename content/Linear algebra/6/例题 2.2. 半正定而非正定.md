二次型 $f\left( {{x}_{1},{x}_{2},\cdots,{x}_{n}}\right) = {x}_{1}^{2} + {x}_{2}^{2} + {x}_{3}^{2} + \cdots + {x}_{n - 1}^{2}$ 是半正定的, 但不是正定二次型. 
实际上, 令 $\left( {{x}_{1},{x}_{2},\cdots,{x}_{n - 1},{x}_{n}}\right) = \left( {0,0,\cdots,0,1}\right) \neq 0$, 则 $f\left( {{x}_{1},{x}_{2},\cdots,{x}_{n}}\right)$ 的值为零.
