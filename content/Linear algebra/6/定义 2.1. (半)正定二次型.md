给定一个实二次型 $f\left( {{x}_{1},{x}_{2},\cdots,{x}_{n}}\right)$. 
若对于任意 $n$ 个不全为零的实数 ${c}_{1},{c}_{2},\cdots,{c}_{n}$, 总有 $f\left( {{c}_{1},{c}_{2},\cdots,{c}_{n}}\right) > 0$, 则称 $f\left( {{x}_{1},{x}_{2},\cdots,{x}_{n}}\right)$ 为**正定二次型**. 
若对于任意 $n$ 个实数 ${c}_{1},{c}_{2},\cdots,{c}_{n}$, 总有 $f\left( {{c}_{1},{c}_{2},\cdots,{c}_{n}}\right) \geq 0$, 则称 $f\left( {{x}_{1},{x}_{2},\cdots,{x}_{n}}\right)$ 为**半正定二次型**.
