设 $f\left( {{x}_{1},{x}_{2},\cdots,{x}_{n}}\right)$ 是一个实二次型, 
- 若对于任意*不全为零*的实数 ${c}_{1},{c}_{2},\cdots,{c}_{n}$, 均有 $$f\left( {{c}_{1},{c}_{2},\cdots,{c}_{n}}\right) < 0 ,$$ 则称 $f\left( {{x}_{1},{x}_{2},\cdots,{x}_{n}}\right)$ 为**负定的**. 
- 如果对于任意 $n$ 个实数 ${c}_{1},{c}_{2},\cdots,{c}_{n}$, 总有 $$f\left( {{c}_{1},{c}_{2},\cdots,{c}_{n}}\right) \leq 0 ,$$ 则称 $f\left( {{x}_{1},{x}_{2},\cdots,{x}_{n}}\right)$ 为**半负定的**.
