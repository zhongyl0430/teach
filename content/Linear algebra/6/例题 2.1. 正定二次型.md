二次型
$$
{f}_{1}\left( {{x}_{1},{x}_{2},\cdots,{x}_{n}}\right) = {x}_{1}^{2} + {x}_{2}^{2} + \cdots + {x}_{n}^{2}
$$
$$
{f}_{2}\left( {{x}_{1},{x}_{2},\cdots,{x}_{n}}\right) = {x}_{1}^{2} + {\left( {x}_{1} + {x}_{2}\right) }^{2} + \cdots + {\left( {x}_{1} + {x}_{2} + \cdots + {x}_{n}\right) }^{2}
$$
都是正定二次型.
