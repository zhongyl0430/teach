> [!theorem] 定理 2.1.1 (随机变量分布函数的特征性质)
> 设 $\left( {\Omega, \mathcal{F}, P}\right)$ 为概率空间, $X$ 为随机变量, 其分布函数为 ${F}_{X}$, 则
> 1. $0 \leq  {F}_{X}\left( x\right)  \leq  1, \forall x \in  \mathbf{R}$.
> 2. 对任 ${x}_{1} < {x}_{2}$, 有 ${F}_{X}\left( {x}_{1}\right)  \leq  {F}_{X}\left( {x}_{2}\right)$, 且 $\mathop{\lim }\limits_{{x \rightarrow  {x}_{1}}}{F}_{X}\left( x\right)  = {F}_{X}\left( {x}_{0}\right)$.
> 3. $\mathop{\lim }\limits_{{x \rightarrow   - \infty }}{F}_{X}\left( x\right)  = 0$, $\mathop{\lim }\limits_{{x \rightarrow  \infty }}{F}_{X}\left( x\right)  = 1$.

^statement
