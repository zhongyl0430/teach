> [!theorem] 定理 1.3.2 (全概率公式)
> 设 $\left( {\Omega, \mathcal{F},  P}\right)$ 为概率空间,  ${B}_{i} \in  \mathcal{F},  P\left( {B}_{i}\right)  > 0,  i =$ $1, 2, \cdots, {B}_{i} \cap  {B}_{j} = \varnothing,  i \neq  j$, 且 $A \subset  \mathop{\bigcup }\limits_{{i = 1}}^{\infty }{B}_{i}$, 则
> $$
> P\left( A\right)  = \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 1}}^{\infty }P\left( {B}_{i}\right) P\left( {A \mid  {B}_{i}}\right). \tag{1.3.5}
> $$

^statement

## 证明 
由 $A \subset  \mathop{\bigcup }\limits_{{i = 1}}^{\infty }{B}_{i}$, 知 $A = A\left( {\mathop{\bigcup }\limits_{{i = 1}}^{\infty }{B}_{i}}\right)$. 
所以, 由 ${B}_{1}, {B}_{2}, \cdots$ 互不相容和概率的可列可加性有
$$
\begin{align}
P(A) &= P\left(A \left( \bigcup_{i=1}^{\infty} B_i \right)\right) \\
     &= P\left(\bigcup_{i=1}^{\infty} A B_i\right) \\
     &= \sum_{i=1}^{\infty} P\left(A B_i\right) \\
     &= \sum_{i=1}^{\infty} P\left(B_i\right) P\left(A \mid B_i\right).
\end{align}
$$
