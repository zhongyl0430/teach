设 $\left( {\Omega, \mathcal{F},  P}\right)$ 为概率空间,  ${A}_{i} \in  \mathcal{F},  i = 1, 2, \cdots,  n$ 且 $P\left( {{A}_{1}{A}_{2}\cdots {A}_{n - 1}}\right)  > 0$, 则

$$
P\left( {{A}_{1}{A}_{2}\cdots {A}_{n}}\right)  = P\left( {A}_{1}\right) P\left( {{A}_{2} \mid  {A}_{1}}\right) P\left( {{A}_{3} \mid  {A}_{1}{A}_{2}}\right) \cdots P\left( {{A}_{n} \mid  {A}_{1}{A}_{2}\cdots {A}_{n - 1}}\right). \tag{1.3.3}
$$

## 证明 
反复应用 (1.3.2),  有
$$
\begin{align}
P(A_1 A_2 \cdots A_n) 
&= P(A_1 A_2 \cdots A_{n-1}) P(A_n \mid A_1 A_2 \cdots A_{n-1}) \\
&= P(A_1 A_2 \cdots A_{n-2}) P(A_{n-1} \mid A_1 A_2 \cdots A_{n-2}) P(A_n \mid A_1 A_2 \cdots A_{n-1}) \\
&= \cdots \\
&= P(A_1 A_2) P(A_3 \mid A_1 A_2) \cdots P(A_n \mid A_1 A_2 \cdots A_{n-1}) \\
&= P(A_1) P(A_2 \mid A_1) P(A_3 \mid A_1 A_2) \cdots P(A_n \mid A_1 A_2 \cdots A_{n-1}).
\end{align}
$$
